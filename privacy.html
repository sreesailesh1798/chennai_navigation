Privacy Policy

App: Chennai Flood & Subway Navigation ("Waze for Chennai Rains")
Effective Date: September 9, 2026
Google Play Store Compliant

1. Introduction

Chennai Flood & Subway Navigation ("the App") is a community-powered public safety and navigation service created to assist commuters in Chennai during monsoons, waterlogging, and cyclone events. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

2. Location Information We Collect

The App collects real-time geographic location data (GPS coordinates) from your device solely to:

  • Calculate safe driving routes that bypass submerged railway subways and flood bowls.
  • Alert you when approaching civic excavations or waterlogged road segments within your immediate driving corridor.
  • Allow you to flag local road flood depths (Ankle, Calf, Knee, Submerged) or active road works to warn other Chennai commuters.

Foreground vs Background Location: Location access is requested primarily while using the App (foreground). Location coordinates are never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers.

3. Community Reports and Crowdsourced Data

When you submit a flood depth report or road work report, the location coordinates, road name, depth level, and timestamp are broadcasted to help other commuters navigate safely. Submissions do not require personal identification (phone numbers, full names, or email addresses).

4. Data Storage and Security

All data transmissions between the App and our backend servers are encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption. Telemetry data is stored securely in structured databases with automated sanitization.

5. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes OpenStreetMap tiles and Leaflet.js for map rendering. These mapping providers may collect anonymous IP addresses for tile delivery without accessing your personal information.

6. User Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to revoke location permissions at any time via your Android device's Settings > Apps > Chennai Flood Nav > Permissions > Location. Revoking permission will allow you to continue using manual map pins and destination searches.

7. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or data inquiries regarding this policy, please contact our community safety team at: privacy@chennaifloodnav.org.
